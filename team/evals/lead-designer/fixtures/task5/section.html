How matching works

Every payment, matched in seconds.

1. Read the chain

We watch your address and read each USDC or USDT transfer: token contract, network, amount.

Checked against the official contract.

2. Match the invoice

Amount and reference are compared with your open invoices. Matched or Not matched.

99.9% of payments match automatically.

3. You approve

Nothing reaches your books until a person approves it.

Then it posts to Xero, QuickBooks and NetSuite.
