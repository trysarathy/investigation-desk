# Sources for the Clearline story

Only these facts are used in the website, mentor brief, film and emails. Each was checked against
the page text on 28 Oct 2026 (the pages' full text is kept alongside this file). Labels: [VERIFIED]
= the quoted line is on the cited page.

## Why now

1. **~$400B real-economy stablecoin payments in 2025, ~60% B2B.** [VERIFIED]
   Bessemer Venture Partners, "Stablecoins: from DeFi primitive to global financial infrastructure",
   published 22 Apr 2026. https://www.bvp.com/atlas/stablecoins-from-defi-primitive-to-global-financial-infrastructure
   > "real-world stablecoin payments volume doubled in 2025 to $400B, 60% of which is estimated to be B2B payments"
   Note: the 60% B2B share is Bessemer's estimate.

2. **Only ~$350–550B of the $28–62T in stablecoin transfers in 2025 were real payments.** [VERIFIED]
   Reap, "Stablecoin Statistics & Data 2026: All You Need To Know", 14 Aug 2026 (citing BIS, BCG × Allium, McKinsey).
   https://reap.global/blog/stablecoin-statistics-2026
   > "Of the $28–62 trillion in stablecoin transfers in 2025, only about $350–550 billion was genuine real-economy payments — the rest is trading and moving funds between wallets and exchanges. (BIS; BCG × Allium; McKinsey)"

3. **Top business use cases: paying suppliers cross-border (62%) and accepting cross-border payments (53%).** [VERIFIED]
   EY-Parthenon 2025 survey of 350 corporate and financial-institution decision-makers, as reported by Reap (same page).
   > "the leading use cases are paying suppliers cross-border (62%) and accepting cross-border payments (53%)."
   Note: mid-2025 survey, before the GENIUS Act was signed; Reap calls it "a directional adoption snapshot". We cite it via Reap; the original EY-Parthenon report was not opened.

4. **The big players are vertically integrating; startups should build outside their paths.** [VERIFIED]
   Bessemer (same article).
   > "Large companies like Stripe, Circle, and Coinbase are leveraging their distribution to become one-stop shops."
   > "We think the most promising early stage startups building today will navigate outside the paths of Stripe, Circle, and Coinbase, and find other mission-critical spaces to generate value."

## Competitors we must acknowledge (checked 28 Oct 2026)

- **Bitwave** says it supports stablecoin invoices and accounts receivable, and syncs payments with
  ERPs "(NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, and more)". https://www.bitwave.io/request-a-demo-stablecoin-b2b-payments [VERIFIED]
- **Request Finance** syncs bills (crypto and fiat) to QuickBooks Online, Xero and NetSuite.
  https://help.request.finance/en/articles/13038304-accounting-integrations-overview [VERIFIED]
So "nobody connects stablecoin payments to the books" is FALSE. Our honest wedge [ASSUMPTION, to test in
customer calls]: smaller businesses that are paid straight into their own wallet, where the hard part is
the payments that arrive wrong (short, wrong network, fake token, no reference) and getting them into
the books correctly, not the happy path.
