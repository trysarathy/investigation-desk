# How to present Clearline (10 minutes)

**Goal of the meeting:** honest feedback on the wedge and 3–5 introductions. Not a sale. Talk for 10 minutes, then listen.

**Before:**
- Open `Clearline_Pitch_Deck.pptx` in PowerPoint (or the PDF as a backup).
- Have `demo_live_full.mp4` ready in a second window, already paused on its first frame.
- Check the sound works.

| Time | Slide | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1 Title | Say who you are and that you're there for feedback. 20 seconds. |
| 0:20 | 2 Problem | Tell Maya's afternoon. Say plainly that "hours" is still an assumption. |
| 1:00 | 3 Why it happens | One sentence per icon. The key line: a transfer has no invoice number. |
| 1:45 | 4 Solution | The one sentence, then the three outcomes. |
| 2:15 | **5 Demo** | **Play the demo here.** Click the video for the 60-second version. If they're engaged, switch to `demo_live_full.mp4` (2 minutes) instead. Then come back to the deck. |
| 4:30 | 6 Why now | Rules, money, rails: one fact each. |
| 5:15 | 7 Who it's for | The two lanes. Say that checkout pages already work well, so you're not competing with them. |
| 6:00 | 8 Competition | Name what each tool does well first. Then the gap. Never say "nobody does this". |
| 6:45 | 9 Insight | Read the three beliefs and say they're untested. |
| 7:15 | 10–11 Model, market | Keep it short. Say these are hypotheses and a small test market. |
| 8:00 | 12 Traction | Be blunt: zero customers, three vendor calls that pushed back. Then the one question and what each answer changes. |
| 9:00 | 13 Team | Ten seconds on you. Spend the time on what's missing. |
| 9:20 | 14 The ask | Ask for the three things, then stop talking. |

**If they ask hard questions:** the appendix slides hold the risks and ten hard questions, each with a one-breath answer. If you don't know, say "I don't know yet, and here's how I'll find out."

**Words to avoid:** revolutionary, seamless, disrupt, "nobody else", "AI-powered". Say "digital dollars (stablecoins)" once, then "digital dollars".

**After:**
- Write down their exact words within an hour.
- Send the follow-up email the same day: the 60-second demo plus one line on what you'll do with their advice.
- Ask for each introduction by name.
