Clearline test-mode console. All organisations, providers, quotes, balances and statements are fictional. No custody, payout execution, lending or compliance decisions. Website · aashmiin.k.2025@accountancy.smu.edu.sg

Guided trial · about 15 minutes

Follow five payments from instruction to evidence.

Clearline shows payment providers what happened to each payout, what evidence supports it, and who must act next. Everything here is fictional test data in your browser.

Open the console

The product in one minute

  • Connect — create a payment intent, compare indicative sandbox routes, approve with two different people, submit, and receive provider events (including timeouts, duplicates, late and contradictory ones).
  • Investigate — each payment keeps three separate states: what the provider reports, what settlement evidence exists, and whether it reconciles. A provider saying “completed” is never shown as “payment successful”.
  • Control — reconcile statement lines by trusted reference, work exceptions with owners and due dates, see usable liquidity under a provider-defined policy, record compliance reviewer decisions, and track funding advances until repaid.

01 / 05 · Successful payment

Workspace · Investigation

Starting state

A supplier payout the sandbox provider reports as completed, plus a separate bank statement line.

What you do

  1. Open the payment.
  2. Read the three status dimensions in the header.
  3. In the evidence timeline, find the provider event and the statement line, each with its source.

Expected system response

  • Execution says the provider reports completion — not “payment successful”.
  • Settlement evidence comes from the bank statement line, matched by trusted reference with code RC_EXACT_REFERENCE_AMOUNT_CURRENCY_WINDOW.
  • Reconciliation is shown separately as matched.

Limitation: The statement line is fictional; no bank is connected.

02 / 05 · Returned payout

Investigation · Exceptions

Starting state

The beneficiary's bank rejected the payout; the provider reported a return and a return line arrived on the statement.

What you do

  1. Open the payment and read the explanation panel.
  2. In the case section, assign an owner and add a note.
  3. Try to resolve the case or its exception.

Expected system response

  • The explanation names what is known, what is not yet known, and what is still financially outstanding.
  • Resolve is refused until the engine's conditions are met, and the reason is shown.
  • Every action appears in the case history and the audit log.

Limitation: Beneficiary details are opaque test references; no real account is checked.

03 / 05 · Compliance hold

Control · Compliance holds

Starting state

The partner placed the payout on hold and asked for supporting documents.

What you do

  1. Submit the requested document names (metadata only).
  2. Assign the compliance reviewer.
  3. Switch “Acting as” to Priya (compliance) and record a decision with a reason.

Expected system response

  • There is no release button; Clearline only records the reviewer's decision.
  • A decision by anyone other than the assigned reviewer is refused with the reason.
  • The hold never releases on a timer or a provider event alone.

Limitation: Clearline makes no compliance decision; the partner and your reviewer do.

04 / 05 · Base Sepolia finality delay

Investigation · Chain evidence

Starting state

A testnet USDC transfer has been observed on Base Sepolia but does not yet have enough confirmations.

What you do

  1. Open the payment and read the chain evidence panel.
  2. Use “Simulate next provider event” to deliver more confirmations.
  3. Watch settlement evidence move from pending finality towards verified.

Expected system response

  • Settlement evidence is pending finality until the confirmation threshold is reached.
  • The scope says on-chain transfer — it never claims a bank credit.
  • Only the allow-listed testnet token on chain 84532 is accepted.

Limitation: Synthetic testnet observations; no live chain lookup in this console.

05 / 05 · Funding exposure

Control · Funding exposure

Starting state

The provider advanced SGD 50,000.00 to pay a supplier before its customer reimbursed it.

What you do

  1. Open Funding exposure.
  2. Check principal, repaid and outstanding, with the due date and repayment evidence.
  3. Follow the exception for the receipt that quoted no reference.

Expected system response

  • SGD 50,000.00 advanced → SGD 30,000.00 repaid by reference → SGD 20,000.00 outstanding.
  • An unreferenced SGD 20,000.00 receipt is an exception, not a close — even though the amount equals what is owed.
  • The supplier payout links to its own investigation page.

Limitation: A projection, not a shared ledger. The provider makes every credit decision; Clearline lends nothing.

Funding Desk and the console

The console's Funding exposure page is a projection from the same engine: it links each advance to the supplier payout it funded, the maker–checker treasury approval, the repayment obligation and the statement lines allocated to it by reference, and computes what is still outstanding. It is not a shared production ledger. The original, more detailed Funding Desk prototype is still available separately.

Open Funding Desk prototype

Honest limitations

  • No licensed provider, bank or chain is connected. Providers, quotes, webhooks, balances and statements are simulated.
  • State lives in this browser only; there is no server persistence, multi-user access or login. Simulated roles stand in for people.
  • Quotes are indicative and never executable. Clearline does not move or hold funds, lend, or make compliance decisions.
  • Not production infrastructure: no SSO, webhook delivery or retries, real settlement attestations or security review yet.
Please do not enter real customer names, bank details, credentials or wallet keys.
