# Two-week, one-provider trial (prepared; nothing sent)

**What this tests.** Whether a provider's operator and reviewer get value from a separate decision-and-exposure record
for supplier payments they fund before the importer pays — without anyone else adopting Clearline. It is a
hypothesis built on one advisor-reported example, not on customer evidence.

**Who is in the room.** The operator who handles these payments, the reviewer who approves funding, and the budget
owner (whoever would pay). No outbound messages are sent by Clearline; the founder runs every session.

**Inputs (authorised, redacted).** Five past cases the provider chooses, including at least **one decline** and **one
late customer receipt**. For each: what was known when, who approved, the payout reference, delivery outcome and
repayment dates. Names may be replaced; amounts and dates kept where the provider permits.

**Days 1–3: baseline.** For each case, the operator walks through how it was handled. We record, labelled
*recorded* or *estimated*: active operator minutes, number of people/messages touched (touchpoints), repeated
evidence requests, request→decision hours, whether the supplier deadline was met, and the outcome.

**Days 4–10: replay in Clearline.** The operator enters each case blind in a blank workspace (no seeded answer); the
reviewer decides in their own sign-in; we use as-of replay to show what was known at the decision. The pilot report
puts Clearline and baseline side by side, every number labelled *computed / recorded / estimated / synthetic / not
measured*, active work separate from elapsed wait.

**Days 11–14: decision.** Review the five reports with the budget owner.

**Price hypothesis (not a quote).** SGD 1,000 for the two-week trial — explicitly a price hypothesis to test
willingness to pay, not an offer.

**What the report never counts as software savings:** capital released, advance principal, interest or fees earned,
or credit risk transferred. Those are financing outcomes, not effects of the software.

**Gates (suggested, not results).**
- Continue: two independent providers share authorised cases; three replays reveal repeated avoidable work; no false
  "fundable / paid / repaid" case survives review; a buyer signs a scoped pilot.
- Pivot: another team (e.g. a lending partner) owns the actual decision.
- Stop expanding: capital or fixed contract terms — not verification — repeatedly bind, and the cheaper existing
  workflow performs as well.
- Never turn a synthetic metric into a customer claim.
