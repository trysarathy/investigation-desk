Data sources

TEST MODE · fictional sandbox data. Provider statuses are provider claims; settlement evidence is shown separately. Clearline does not move money, hold funds or make compliance or credit decisions.
  • Providers: sandbox adapters only (Northline Payments (sandbox), Harbour FX (sandbox), Tidewater Digital (testnet sandbox)). No licensed partner is connected.
  • Quotes: indicative sandbox quotes, never executable.
  • Statements: fictional bank and provider settlement lines entered in this console.
  • Chain: synthetic Base Sepolia testnet observations; an on-chain transfer never proves a bank credit.
  • Funding: a projection linking a payment to an advance, not a shared ledger. Detailed prototype at /mentor-demo.
  • Storage: your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server; reset clears it.

Do not enter real customer names, bank details, credentials or wallet keys.
