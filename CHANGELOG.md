## 2026-11-02
- **"Paid" wording that says what was checked (S1, building block; not on screen yet).** A new set of five checks decides the words shown when a payment is paid: final on the chain, the issuer's real token contract, the exact amount, the sender against the US OFAC sanctions list's published crypto addresses, and Tether's or Circle's freeze list (the sender and your receiving address). The words only claim what passed: "Paid and final. Right token and amount. Sender not on the US OFAC SDN list. Not frozen." If a check couldn't run it says so, e.g. "Sender NOT checked."; if one failed it starts "Don't release:" and names it (wrong token, short amount, wrong network, a look-alike receiving address). Overpaid isn't "Don't release": it says how much more was paid and to check with finance before refunding. It never says "safe". These words are for your staff only: the sanctions and freeze lines are never shown to the payer. "Final" comes from the network's own finality signal (Tron's irreversible block; Ethereum's finalized block), with the payment re-checked on the chain at that moment, never a block count; if that can't be read the words say "Finality NOT checked." and don't call the payment paid. Through Etherscan, which Clearline uses for Ethereum when a key is set, the finalized block can't be read yet, so Ethereum reads "Finality NOT checked." for now. Today the sanctions list copy isn't loaded yet, so the sender always reads "NOT checked". This is not a compliance screening service. Nothing to do on your side.

## 2026-11-01
- **One plain sentence per payment, now on screen (part 2).** Each request's page shows its status sentence under the title, and All requests shows it in a "What happened and what to do" column, e.g. "Short by 50.00 USDC: ask the customer for the rest, or accept it as settled." The line on the request page is marked as a status region, so screen readers can announce it when it changes (not yet tried with a screen reader). An empty filter now says what's empty and links to all requests; while requests load you see a "Loading requests" placeholder; and if requests can't be shown, the page says so in plain words and offers Reload, instead of a technical error. Nothing to do on your side.
- **One plain sentence per payment (part 1: the wording).** Each payment request now has one plain sentence saying what happened and what to do, e.g. "Short by 50.00 USDC: ask the customer for the rest, or accept it as settled." There is no countdown: Clearline only updates a real payment when you look it up again, so while one is confirming the sentence gives Ethereum's usual pace ("Ethereum usually takes about 2 minutes") and asks you to look it up again; it is not a promise of when yours will be done. The words say "confirmed", not "final", because Clearline doesn't yet use Ethereum's own finality signal. Also fixed: the request page said "about about 2 minutes" and called 12 confirmations "final for practical purposes". Nothing to do on your side.
- **The At a glance screen no longer says payments are paid automatically outside the demo.** Automatic marking is a demo-only rule, but the live screen showed a "Paid automatically" tile, a "time saved" estimate and the line "Every payment is matched, checked and explained". In live mode the tile now reads "Checked and ready to mark paid" (payments waiting for you), the time-saved estimate is gone, and the "I've asked for the balance" hint, next step and customer reply say a person marks the invoice paid: nothing is marked paid until you confirm. The demo keeps its wording, now labelled demo. Nothing to do on your side.
