**Hartwell Lane LLP — Insights** · "The GENIUS Act: what happens when" · updated 2 Sep 2026
URL: https://hartwell-lane-law.example/insights/genius-act-timeline

- The Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act (GENIUS Act) was signed
  into law on **18 July 2025** (Public Law 119-27).
- **Effective date:** the earlier of (a) **18 months after enactment (18 January 2027)** or (b)
  **120 days after the primary federal regulators issue final implementing regulations**. Regulators
  were directed to issue rules within one year of enactment.
- The Act regulates **issuers** of "payment stablecoins" (who may issue, reserves, disclosures, no
  interest or yield paid to holders) and **digital asset service providers** (exchanges, custodians
  and similar intermediaries).
- **Three years after enactment (18 July 2028)** it becomes unlawful for a digital asset service
  provider to offer or sell to a person in the United States a payment stablecoin that is not issued by
  a permitted payment stablecoin issuer, subject to a pathway for foreign issuers from jurisdictions
  Treasury finds comparable.
- "The Act does not, by its terms, make it unlawful for an ordinary business to receive a stablecoin as
  payment. Businesses should nonetheless expect their exchanges, custodians and banks to change which
  stablecoins they support." (Our view; not legal advice.)
- As of this update we are tracking proposed rules from several agencies; we are not aware of all final
  rules having been issued.
