# 5-minute talk track: the 10-slide deck

About 30 seconds a slide. Speak the lines below, and don't read the slide. The demo video (slide 4) is optional in a 5-minute slot. If you play the 60-second cut, drop the "Say" on slides 3 and 9 to make up the time.

| Time | Slide | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **1 Title** | "I'm Aashmiin, a second-year SMU accountancy student. This is Clearline, a working demo. I'm here for honest feedback, not to sell." |
| 0:20 | **2 Paid in seconds, checked for hours** | "When a business is paid in stablecoins, the money arrives in seconds. When something's off, a finance person spends a long time finding out which of these four it was. 'Hours' is my assumption; interviews will test it." |
| 0:55 | **3 Smart cashier** | "Clearline tells the customer exactly how to pay, has correct payments ready to mark paid once you approve, and explains problems in plain English with the reply drafted. It never touches the money." |
| 1:20 | **4 Demo** | "This is the real app on fictional data." Point at 1, 2, 3. *Optional: click the video (60 s).* |
| 1:50 | **5 Why now** | "Singapore is writing its stablecoin rules now. McKinsey and Artemis estimate about US$226 billion a year of business stablecoin payments, though that's still tiny. And Singapore is a top-five sender." |
| 2:25 | **6 Direct payments are the gap** | Point at the two lanes. "Checkout pages handle their own payments. Direct transfers from exchanges and company wallets land on a person." Then the table: "Every piece exists somewhere; nobody I've found handles the exception and the reply. That's a belief, not a finding." |
| 3:10 | **7 Pricing and market** | "Two untested pricing bets. A deliberately small Singapore test market: a ceiling of about S$0.9 million. I'll size beyond Singapore only after interviews." |
| 3:40 | **8 Traction** | "Honestly: a working product, 52 of 52 held-out real transactions given the right verdict, zero customers. My three vendor calls pushed back. Next is ten finance teams, and one question: checkout page or direct? Direct means I sell to finance teams; checkout means I become a plug-in for gateways." |
| 4:20 | **9 Team** | "Just me: I build it, with AI coding tools, and I do the customer work. What I'm missing is payments-operations experience." |
| 4:40 | **10 The ask** | "Three things: your honest view of the wedge, introductions to three to five finance teams, and advice on gateways. Thank you." Then stop talking. |

**If they ask:** Backup A covers the risks and Backup B the hard questions. The full answers to all ten hard questions are in Backup B's speaker notes.
