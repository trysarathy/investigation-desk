Note title: "GENIUS Act — key sections (paraphrase)" · saved by research-intern 2026-09-12
Source cited in the note: congress.gov text of S.1582 (enrolled), Public Law 119-27.

- Sec. 3: only permitted payment stablecoin issuers may issue a payment stablecoin in the US. From 3
  years after enactment, a digital asset service provider may not offer or sell a payment stablecoin
  to a person in the US unless issued by a permitted issuer (foreign-issuer pathway exists, with
  conditions incl. a Treasury comparability determination).
- Sec. 4: reserves 1:1 in specified assets; monthly reserve disclosure; issuers may not pay interest
  or yield to holders.
- Effective date section: earlier of 18 months after enactment or 120 days after final regulations.
- Rulemaking: regulators to promulgate regulations within 1 year of enactment.
