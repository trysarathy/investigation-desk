# First learning batch: 5 interviews, three separate signals

Report each signal on its own. Never add them together, and never count software tests as evidence.

| Signal | Question | Counts as a yes only if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Usability** | Could the operator tell Friday's evidence from hindsight? | In Task A they stated a Friday decision without using the 78.77 figure, and in Task B they named something learned later. In Task C they told "need better information" (stale balance) apart from "short of cash" (forecast receipt). |
| **Behaviour** | Did an authorised treasury owner agree to share a redacted week of obligations and funding records? | A named person with authority said yes to a specific week, with agreed redaction and deletion. "Maybe later" is a no. |
| **Commercial** | Did a budget owner agree to a scoped paid pilot at an explicit price? | A budget owner agreed to a defined scope (e.g. one corridor, four weeks) at a stated price (e.g. SGD 500). Interest without a price is a no. |

## Validation gates (unchanged; none met yet)
1. Two unrelated firms share permitted data.
2. At least three genuinely discretionary funding decisions across them.
3. A distinct paid-pilot test.

## Read the answers honestly (keep these as separate findings)
- **Funding dates are contractual** (the provider or bank sets them) → the pain is financing or renegotiation, not
  timing evidence. Note it; don't build for it.
- **They already pick the same date with a spreadsheet or calendar** → no demonstrated reason to buy.
- **The repeated delay is waiting to release goods after a receipt arrives** → evidence for the earlier
  Clearline receipt-checking hypothesis. Log it separately.
- **The team chose early funding because they weren't sure**, and a later date was allowed → the signal this test
  looks for.

## After interview 1, ask yourself
**"Did this person choose the funding date, and could they have chosen a later one?"** If the date was fixed by
contract or nobody in the room decides it, find a different kind of interviewee before running session 2.
