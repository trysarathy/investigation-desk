# Script: OTC desk (intro offered by Vishal)

Use with `SCRIPT_CORE.md`. Replaces the core's story block. Who: a trader, sales or ops person at an
OTC desk that converts fiat to/from stablecoins for business clients. DRAFT, 2026-11-02.
**They are a partner/channel, not a buyer.** What they tell you about clients is **second-hand**: log
source = advisor; it guides who to call next but never counts as a customer Strong on its own.
Main hypotheses: **H3, H2, H1** (what their business clients struggle with). Never ask for client names
they can't share; ask for intros instead. Never ask about their own compliance in a way that sounds like
an audit; we're learning.

| # | Question | Tests | Supports if they say… | Refutes if they say… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Tell me about the last business client you onboarded who receives stablecoins from their own customers. What were they trying to do?" | context/H1 | Merchants/exporters paid in USDC/USDT who then convert | Clients are only traders/funds, no operating businesses |
| 2 | "When that client's money comes in, what do they ask you for before they act (release goods, pay suppliers)? Last time?" | H1 | Clients ask the desk to confirm receipts; confirmation delays | Clients never ask; they have their own tools |
| 3 | "What questions did clients ask you last month about records for their accountant or auditor? What did you give them?" | H2/H4 | Requests for trade confirms, rates, statements for books; desk sends spreadsheets | Nobody asks |
| 4 | "Which of your clients keep books in a currency other than USD? What do they struggle with when they convert?" | H2 | SGD/EUR/INR clients; FX gain/loss questions; which rate to use | All USD |
| 5 | "Tell me about a recent client that uses stablecoins to avoid pre-funding a cross-border payout. How did they set it up, and what went wrong?" | H3 | Real cases, amounts, corridors; friction points | None; rare |
| 6 | "What's the most common thing that goes wrong on a client's incoming transfer (wrong network, short, wrong token, unreferenced)? What did you do last time?" | H1/H4 | Frequency, tickets, time spent by the desk | Rare/never |
| 7 | "How much of your ops team's time last month went to helping clients with these issues rather than trading?" | H1/H2 | Their number, a named role | None |
| 8 | "What tools or partners do your clients use today for accounting or reconciliation? Any they've complained about?" | H2/H4 | Names + complaints (for research-intern; label as second-hand) | Happy with X |
| 9 | "Have you ever been asked to provide something you couldn't, e.g. a per-lot cost record or a payment confirmation for ops?" | H1/H2 | Specific unmet asks | No |
| 10 | "Which two or three of your clients feel this most? Would you be willing to introduce me to one?" | advance | Intro offered with a name/type | Declines |

Never: suggest a partnership, referral fee, integration or anything commercial. If they raise one: "That's
kind; I'll come back to you once I understand the problem better" (any commercial step is Aashmiin's).
