# Two weeks to test funding discretion

**Decision:** Is there a paid, repeatable decision about *when* to fund one corridor? A need for additional money is a different problem. Target controllers at Singapore food importers paying US suppliers in USD. No company named in the whitepaper has agreed to participate.

## Days 1–3: reconstruct a real payout

Start with six qualifying conversations across independent firms, including a buyer who is happy with its current process. Ask an SMU accountancy alumnus, a BIG founder/adviser and the PV contact who offered introductions for referrals to AP and treasury signatories. If no genuine connection exists, request a referral through the prospect's published company channel. Draft only:

> I’m an SMU accountancy student studying how Singapore importers decide when to fund overseas supplier payments. Could the person who prepares your payment run walk me through one recent USD payout for 20 minutes? A case where the process worked well would be equally useful. No account numbers or client names are needed.

Begin: **“Walk me through the last payout for which you had to move cash earlier than you wanted.”** If the premise does not fit: “Walk me through your most recent overseas supplier payment, from approval until your supplier could use the money.”

1. What amount or range, currency, legal entity, destination, due date and time zone applied?
2. When did usable cash leave your control? When did the beneficiary actually have usable funds? Who can attest to each?
3. What contract, bank cutoff, limit, weekend or internal policy set the last safe funding time? Who could change it?
4. What did the operator actually know at that earlier time? What was merely forecast? What else had claims on the same balance?
5. What alternative did you try? What did the spreadsheet, bank or provider already tell you?
6. Did anyone borrow, earn less, pay extra fees or miss a delivery? Who bore the cost? Which evidence distinguishes a cost from an idle balance?
7. May we replay a redacted week, including successful and unsuccessful cases, locally under agreed terms? Who can authorise data access and a paid test?

## Days 4–7: collect, minimise and freeze

Seek two independent organisations, one authorised week each. Collect pseudonymous invoice IDs, amounts or ranges, recorded balances/encumbrances, effective and first-known timestamps, terms, calendars, bank funding records, supplier-availability evidence and actual decisions. Remove unnecessary names, account numbers and unrelated transactions. Agree permitted use, retention and deletion before receipt. If only final statements exist, label decision-time evidence missing; do not backfill it into Friday.

## Days 8–11: compare like for like

Replay complete corridor-days. Record (a) actual process, (b) best inexpensive calendar/contract rule, (c) candidate. Keep the same net amount, destination, date, guarantee level, reserves and FX protection. Set aside a later contiguous period if enough records exist; otherwise label the result exploratory. Log unknowns, false reassurance, emergency top-ups, fees, staff minutes and missed cutoffs. Show friday.html only after reconstructing the incident. Ask what would actually change and how enforceable capacity would be obtained.

## Days 12–14: ask for a decision

**Behavioural pass:** two unrelated treasury owners share a permitted week; identify at least three genuinely discretionary decisions across them. **Commercial test:** one budget holder signs a four-week SGD 500 shadow-pilot scope, agreeing to examine a subsequent SGD 270/month offer only against measured net value. Target at least SGD 540/month of verified gross benefit after incremental operating costs and risk, before subscription. These are targets, not results.

**Pause** any configuration that would breach a floor or deadline. **Pivot** if the cause is supplier terms, credit need, receipt attribution or FX recordkeeping. Require direct evidence from the appropriate buyer before pursuing that different product. **Stop development** if the inexpensive control matches performance, no qualified owner can change funding time, or the completed scoped offer produces neither usable data nor payment. If interviews were not obtained, report access failure, not market rejection.
