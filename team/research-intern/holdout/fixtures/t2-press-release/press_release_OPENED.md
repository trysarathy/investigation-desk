# Ferrous Pay raises US$12M Series A as stablecoin volume passes US$2.1B in Q3
**SINGAPORE, 27 October 2026** — Ferrous Pay, the leading stablecoin payments platform for businesses
in Asia, today announced a US$12 million Series A led by Larch Capital (FICTIONAL).

- "More than **US$2.1 billion** in stablecoin volume processed in Q3 2026, up 340% year on year."
- "Trusted by **1,200+ businesses** across 14 markets."
- "The **first fully automated** stablecoin reconciliation platform, with **native sync to Xero,
  QuickBooks and NetSuite**."
- "AI-powered matching with **99.9% accuracy**."
- "Built on **MAS-licensed** payment rails."

"Finance teams should never have to reconcile a stablecoin payment by hand again," said CEO
Lena Hartono (FICTIONAL).

*About Ferrous Pay:* Ferrous Pay Pte. Ltd. provides stablecoin acceptance, conversion and payout
services. Regulated payment services in Singapore are provided by our licensed partner, Corvane
Payments Pte. Ltd. (FICTIONAL), a Major Payment Institution licensed by the Monetary Authority of
Singapore. "Volume processed" includes incoming payments, outgoing payouts, conversions and transfers
between customer wallets. Accuracy figure based on internal testing.
