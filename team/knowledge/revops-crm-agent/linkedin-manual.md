## 1. The rule
**All LinkedIn steps are human.** You never log in to LinkedIn, never use a browser extension, bot,
scheduler, scraper, "LinkedIn automation" SaaS (Phantombuster, Dripify, Expandi, Waalaxy, lemlist LinkedIn
steps, etc.), never export or download contacts, never send a message or invitation. You prepare a list; she
clicks. This holds even if she asks you to automate: explain why and offer the manual pack.

## 2. Why: LinkedIn User Agreement
Source: LinkedIn User Agreement, section 8.2 "Don'ts", https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement

https://contentin.io/blog/linkedin-mcp-terms-of-service/ , https://getfuzzy.ai/blog/linkedin-tos-automation-guide].

  or redirect messages";
- (also) copy profiles or other data, or override security features or access limits.
Enforcement described as graduated: warning, temporary restriction, longer restriction, permanent ban
[ASSUMPTION: search-only]. A restricted account would cost Aashmiin her main founder channel and her

## 3. Limits to plan around (unofficial; LinkedIn doesn't publish them)
[ASSUMPTION: search-only; https://www.hublead.io/linkedin-character-limit ,
https://help.dripify.io/en/articles/8490987-limited-personalized-connection-request-notes-for-free-linkedin-accounts ,
https://help.lemlist.com/en/articles/8514157-linkedin-connection-requests-limits-best-practices-avoiding-restrictions]
- Connection **note: 200 characters** on free accounts per a Sept 2026 check (older sources say 300; Premium
  300). Write notes **≤ 200 characters** so they work on any plan.
- **Free accounts may add a note to only a few invitations per month** (sources say 3-10; most recent says
  3). Invitations without a note aren't limited by this. If she's on a free plan, put the note-worthy people
  first and give the rest a **no-note** invitation plus a DM after they accept.
- **Weekly invitation ceiling ~100** (rolling 7 days); many practitioners advise ≤ 20-30/day. Our daily pack:
  **≤ 15 connection requests and ≤ 15 DMs** so she can do it in 15 minutes and stay far below the ceiling
  [ASSUMPTION: our choice].
- DMs (messages to 1st-degree connections) have a long limit; keep ours ≤ 500 characters anyway.

## 5. Formats
**Connection note (≤ 200 characters, no link, no pitch):**
"Hi Katrin, I read Feldmann now accepts USDC from Asian freight partners. I'm an SMU student researching how
finance teams handle stablecoin payments. Glad to connect." (≈ 165 characters)
**DM 1 (after acceptance, ≤ 500 characters, one question):**
"Thanks for connecting, Katrin. I'm researching what happens when stablecoin payments arrive wrong (short,
wrong network, no invoice reference). When a USDC payment comes in short, who sorts it out at Feldmann, and
how long does it take? 15 minutes of your experience would help me a lot. I'm not selling anything."
**DM 2 (7 days after DM 1, only if no reply; last one):** the accounting question word for word, plus "No
worries if not, thank you either way."
Never a link in a connection note; never attachments; never claim a mutual contact you haven't verified.
