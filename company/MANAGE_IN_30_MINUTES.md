# Managing your AI team in 30 minutes a day

Your team works overnight and through the day. Your job is the things only you can do: decide,
approve, talk to customers. Everything arrives in your inbox from aashmiin@gmail.com, subject
starting "Clearline".

## Every morning (15 minutes, after 07:30)
1. **Open "Clearline briefing <date>"** and read the top section first. Red items (a failed task, a
   risk, a bounce spike, PAUSE) come before anything else.
2. **Clear the approval queue.** Reply to the briefing with your answers, numbered:
   `1 yes, 2 no, 3 yes but change X`. The chief-of-staff records them and the right employee acts on
   the next run. Merge the PRs QA marked green on GitHub yourself (you're the only one who merges).
3. **Make the 3 decisions.** Each has a recommendation; "agree" is a fine answer.

## During the day (10 minutes)
4. **LinkedIn (by hand).** Open "Clearline LinkedIn pack <date>", send the connection notes and DMs,
   then set each row's status in the LinkedIn list tab. No tool does this for you, on purpose.
5. **Calls.** The briefing has a prep brief for each call. After the call, drop 3-5 lines of notes in
   the Calls tab. The evening run turns them into a summary, a follow-up draft and roadmap rows.

## Evening (5 minutes, weekdays after 18:24)
6. **Skim "Clearline evening summary".** Tomorrow's first emails are in it in full. Reply `HOLD` to
   stop tomorrow's batch, or do nothing to let them go.

## Once a week
- **Monday**: approve or edit the week plan (reply to "Clearline week plan").
- **Friday**: merge what the ship review says is ready.
- **Sunday**: read the weekly wrap; edit and send the mentor update yourself if you want to.

## Controls
| You want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Stop everything | Reply `PAUSE` to any Clearline email, or set Settings PAUSE = TRUE |
| Restart | Set Settings PAUSE = FALSE |
| Stop tomorrow's first emails only | Reply `HOLD` to the evening summary |
| Give an employee work | Add a row to their tab (see AGENTS.md), or reply to the briefing |
| Change a schedule | claude.ai → Scheduled tasks, or ask any Claude session |
| Ask one employee something now | In Claude Code on investigation-desk: "Use the <name> agent to ..." |

## What they will never do without you
Email someone new beyond the queue you saw; write to customers, mentors or investors; quote prices,
send invoices, sign or pay anything; merge code; delete data. If you see any of these happen, reply
PAUSE and tell the chief-of-staff what you saw.
