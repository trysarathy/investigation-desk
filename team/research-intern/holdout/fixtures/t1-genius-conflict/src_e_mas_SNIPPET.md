mas.gov.sg · "MAS finalises stablecoin regulatory framework" · 15 Aug 2023
"...The framework will apply to single-currency stablecoins (SCS) pegged to the Singapore Dollar or any
G10 currency, that are issued in Singapore... Issuers that meet all requirements can apply to have their
stablecoins recognised as 'MAS-regulated stablecoins'... MAS will not prohibit the issuance and use of
other stablecoins in Singapore, but users should be aware they do not enjoy the same protections..."
