# My daily 60 minutes as CEO

The team runs the company. You spend 60 minutes a day running the team, and the rest of your day on
the things only you can do: being the face of Clearline, talking to buyers, and closing.
Everything arrives from aashmiin@gmail.com, with a subject starting "Clearline".

| When (SGT) | Minutes | What you do | What's waiting for you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | 15 | **Briefing + approvals.** Read the red items at the top first (risks, PAUSE, a red build). Reply with numbered answers to the approval queue (`1 yes, 2 no, 3 yes but change X`) and the 3 decisions ("agree" is a fine answer). | "Clearline briefing <date>" from chief-of-staff |
| 07:45 | 10 | **Call prep.** Read the brief for each call today: who they are, the people, likely objections, the demo path. | Prep Docs from sales-closer-assistant (ae-assistant until week 2) |
| Midday | 10 | **LinkedIn by hand.** Send today's connection notes and follow-up DMs, approve your founder post. Mark each row sent in the LinkedIn list tab. | "Clearline LinkedIn pack" from revops-crm-agent; post drafts from content |
| After each call | 5 | **Call notes.** 3-5 lines in the Calls tab: their pain in their words, how they do it today, volume, next step. Everything downstream (follow-up, proposal, roadmap) is built from these. | Follow-up draft appears the same evening |
| 18:30 | 10 | **End of day.** Skim it, send the follow-up drafts you like, and read tomorrow's first emails (in full). Reply `HOLD` to stop tomorrow's batch; do nothing to let it go. | "Clearline end of day <date>" from chief-of-staff |
| Any time | 10 | **Merges.** Merge only what QA marked green and the ship review lists as ready. You're the only one who merges. | PRs on investigation-desk |

## Once a week (instead of that day's merges and end-of-day skim)
- **Monday 08:45** (15 min): approve or edit the week plan: 3 outcomes, each employee's targets, your tasks.
- **Friday 17:15** (15 min): the ship review. What shipped, what to merge, next week's build (you set `next`).
- **Sunday 17:40** (15 min): the weekly report. Edit the advisor update draft and send it yourself.
- **1st of the month**: edit the investor-style update draft and send it yourself.

## What only you do
Anything to customers, investors, advisors or mentors; prices, proposals, contracts, invoices; posts
in your name; merging to `main`; spending money; deleting data; unlocking more outreach. The team
prepares all of it and waits for your yes.

## Controls
| You want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Stop everything | Reply `PAUSE` to any Clearline email, or set Settings PAUSE = TRUE |
| Restart | Set Settings PAUSE = FALSE |
| Stop tomorrow's first emails only | Reply `HOLD` to the end-of-day email |
| Give someone work | Reply to the briefing, or add a row (Roadmap, Decisions log, Targets) |
| Ask one employee something now | In Claude Code on investigation-desk: "Use the <name> agent to ..." |
| See who does what | `TEAM.md` |

If you ever see the team do something on the "only you" list, reply PAUSE and tell the chief-of-staff.
