# Provider discovery: distinguish customer credit from prefunding

Research date: 6 November 2026. Owner: Codex, using the research-intern and gtm-strategist briefs. This is desk research, not a customer survey. The supplied competitor pack's 7 November date is later than the session date; it is not treated as a verification date here.

Singapore office presence does not establish local eligibility for a global credit product.

## Corrections that matter

- [VERIFIED] [Tazapay's May 2026 article](https://tazapay.com/blog/how-no-prefunding-payouts-work) describes funding each payment when initiated. Its [September 2026 wallet terms](https://tazapay.com/en-sg/legal/seller/appendix-3-tazapay-payouts-terms-and-conditions) require sufficient balances and disallow negative balances. [ASSUMPTION] These can describe different funding products; neither proves credit to a customer. Ask about specific contracts rather than declare a contradiction or a lending facility.
- [VERIFIED] [Flash Payments help](https://help.flash-payments.com/en/articles/999420-why-do-i-have-to-deposit-money-before-i-can-transfer) requires cash in the account before confirmation. [ASSUMPTION] Its general published workflow is poor evidence for Clearline's advance buyer. Institutional exceptions are unknown. The older help article must be reconfirmed with an operator.
- [VERIFIED] [Airwallex transfer documentation](https://www.airwallex.com/docs/global-treasury/process-payouts/create-a-transfer) permits unfunded booking in a post-funding workflow but processes transfers once funded. [ASSUMPTION] Booking risk is different from a supplier paid while a customer still owes principal.
- [VERIFIED] [WorldFirst's credit documentation](https://docs.worldfirst.com/wfdocs/products/embedded_finance_product_types), dated 29 September 2026, includes supplier card-credit payments and repayment APIs. [ASSUMPTION] This strengthens the substitution threat; market eligibility and the financing legal entity still need confirmation. Do not describe it as generally available Singapore lending.
- [VERIFIED] [Volopay's credit repayment help](https://help.volopay.co/en/articles/4746743-credit-line-repayments) labels the capability legacy-only. [ASSUMPTION] A credit mention alone cannot qualify a new customer cohort.
- [VERIFIED] [Thunes](https://www.thunes.com/insights/learn/stablecoin-prefunding), [Nium](https://docs.nium.com/docs/payins/program-client-and-client-prefund-account) and [Tranglo's company filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1862935/000149315225009301/forms-1a.htm) describe prefunding. [ASSUMPTION] Corridor liquidity and customer receivables must be separate discovery questions.
