# Clearline roadmap: provider advances

[ASSUMPTION: founder's current brief] A desk for payment providers that pay suppliers before their customers reimburse them: review the advance, then track what is still owed. The provider makes credit decisions and supplies funds. Clearline's software should preserve evidence and identify exceptions; it does not supply credit or custody.

This is a proposed ordering for founder review, not a delivery commitment or a claim of customer validation. Sources: [mentor review, top ten](mentor-review/REVIEW.md#top-ten-fixes-in-credibility-order), [mentor ask](mentor-review/MENTOR_SHEET.md), [validation limits](mentor-review/VALIDATION.md), supplied [competitor pack](research/COMPETITORS.md), and [primary-source recheck](research/COMPETITOR_RECHECK.md). ## Stop doing

- [ASSUMPTION: founder scope] Pause stablecoin invoice matching as public positioning and new-chain growth; keep its code and finish isolated safety fixes.

- [ASSUMPTION] Stop treating providers' rail prefunding as proof of customer credit, and stop calling unqualified prospects design partners.
- [ASSUMPTION] Stop adding a feature without naming the operator's action, required evidence and measurable improvement over their sheet or existing system.
- [ASSUMPTION] Stop presenting synthetic state coverage as customer accuracy, test totals from separate branches as combined coverage, or a successful deploy status as a verified live journey.
