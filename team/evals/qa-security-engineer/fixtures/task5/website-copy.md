# Proposed homepage copy for the website relaunch (draft from content-marketer, 29 Oct 2026)

## Hero
**Stablecoin payments, reconciled for you.**
Clearline watches your wallets in real time, matches every USDC and USDT payment to its invoice and
posts to Xero automatically. Trusted by 50 finance teams across Asia.

## How it works
1. **Send a payment page.** Each invoice gets its own page with a QR code. USDC and USDT, on Ethereum
   and Tron.
2. **We check every transfer.** Clearline checks the token is the real one from the issuer's own
   contract, so a counterfeit can't be marked paid by mistake.
3. **Approve and it's in the books.** Nothing is written to your books until a person approves.
   Every posting is kept in an audit log.

## Security
- Bank-grade encryption for all your data, at rest and in transit.
- SOC 2 Type II in progress.
- Read-only: Clearline never holds, moves or has access to your funds, and never asks for wallet keys.
- Tested: 573 automated tests pass on every release.

## FAQ
**How long does set-up take?** About 5 minutes.
**Does it use AI?** Our AI decides which invoice each payment belongs to.
**Is it live?** Yes, with customers in Singapore and Hong Kong.
