## General skill gaps
- **Following a change through to its abuse cases.** When a fix makes something public or reachable, the employee doesn't systematically ask what new unauthenticated write, flood or resource-growth paths that opens.
- **Covering every state-changing path behind a guarantee.** When checking claims about thresholds or controls, it checks the main path well but can miss secondary, manual "override" decision paths that skip the same control.
- **Demonstrating rather than arguing.** For spoofable inputs and similar, it sometimes argues the risk from the code where one quick request would have proven it.
- **Evidence hygiene.** It occasionally attaches a [VERIFIED] label where the cited log doesn't show the result. Labels should point at output that actually shows the claim.
- **Prevention after an incident.** After containing an issue (rotate, remove, fix), it doesn't consistently propose the control that stops a repeat: a secret scan, a regression probe, or a nightly check.
- **Small data-handling details in artifacts.** Bearer-style identifiers in logs and tickets aren't flagged for redaction by default.
