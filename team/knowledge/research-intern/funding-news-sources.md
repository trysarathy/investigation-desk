## Company primary (P2) — competitors and leads

## Searches that work (copy-paste)
- `site:occ.gov stablecoin` · `site:esma.europa.eu MiCA statement` · `site:hkma.gov.hk stablecoin`
  · `site:mas.gov.sg stablecoin` · `site:fca.org.uk stablecoin`
- `"stablecoin" "raises" OR "Series A" OR "seed" <month year>` then open the PR.
- `"<competitor>" pricing OR launches OR acquires` restricted to the last 7 days.

## Do not use as a source
X/Telegram posts without a primary link; AI-generated news sites (e.g. pages whose byline is
missing and which conflict with primary sources); price-prediction sites; anything behind a login.
