# Payment edge-case catalogue

What Clearline must do with each awkward payment, whether a test proves it today, and the test to add.
Read against `origin/main` @ `fd9b992` on 29 Oct 2026. Behaviour marked **probe** was confirmed by
running the real code in a scratch copy that day (a throwaway test, not committed) [VERIFIED].
Product scope reminder: payment requests are **Ethereum only, USDC and USDT**
(`src/lib/requests/types.ts` `REQUEST_CHAIN`, `REQUEST_TOKENS`); the older deposit-investigation
engine covers Tron, Ethereum, BNB Chain and cross-checks Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism.

Principles every case follows (from `docs/SPEC.md` §2 and §6, and the code):
1. **Never count, mark paid or post on a missing or ambiguous fact.** Unknown → wait or "Check paused".
2. **A heuristic never hides a fact** (look-alike is a finding on top of "wrong address").
3. **Only a timing flag can be overridden by a person**; counterfeit, failed, wrong network and wrong
   token never count (`lifecycle.ts` `transferIssue`, `accounting/rules.ts` `neverKinds`).
4. **Exact decimals only**: amounts are strings; no floats (`decimal.ts`, `payments/money.ts`).
5. **Nothing reaches the books until a person approves the exact plan they saw** (plan key).
