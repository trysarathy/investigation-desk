## 2026-11-02
- **Demo only: the matched sample invoice and the entry Clearline would propose (/demo/entry, demo mode only).** The matched sample invoice with the Xero entry Clearline would propose; Approve stays disabled until the payment is final (no sample is final with an entry today) and never sends anything. The amounts come from the existing posting rules, unchanged. Nothing to do on your side.
- **Two demo-only screens and a demo watcher for the end of discovery calls (/demo, demo mode only).** A release card that says "Paid and final", "Not final yet" or "Don't release: …" using only the checks that ran (today the sender always reads "NOT checked", and Ethereum payments read "Finality NOT checked."; the Tron sample reads "Paid and final" from a recorded finality read); and an email and a Slack-style preview of the same result, quiet unless a payment is paid and final or has a problem ("Preview — nothing is sent"). A watcher replays recorded transfers on a timer ("Demo: replaying recorded transfers"); it takes no address and makes no live call. All transfers are real public ones replayed offline; all invoices are samples. Live mode is unchanged. Nothing to do on your side.
- **"Paid" wording that says what was checked (S1, building block; not on screen yet).** A new set of five checks decides the words shown when a payment is paid: final on the chain, the issuer's real token contract, the exact amount, the sender against the US OFAC sanctions list's published crypto addresses, and Tether's or Circle's freeze list (the sender and your receiving address). The words only claim what passed: "Paid and final. Right token and amount. Sender not on public sanctions lists. Not frozen." If a check couldn't run it says so, e.g. "Sender NOT checked."; if one failed it starts "Don't release:" and names it (wrong token, short amount, wrong network, a look-alike receiving address). Overpaid isn't "Don't release": it says how much more was paid and to check with finance before refunding. It never says "safe". "Final" comes from the network's own finality signal (Tron's irreversible block; Ethereum's finalized block), with the payment re-checked on the chain at that moment, never a block count; if that can't be read the words say "Finality NOT checked." and don't call the payment paid. Through Etherscan, which Clearline uses for Ethereum when a key is set, the finalized block can't be read yet, so Ethereum reads "Finality NOT checked." for now. Today the sanctions list copy isn't loaded yet, so the sender always reads "NOT checked". This is not a compliance screening service. Nothing to do on your side.

## 2026-11-01
- **One plain sentence per payment, now on screen (part 2).** Each request's page shows its status sentence under the title, and All requests shows it in a "What happened and what to do" column, e.g. "Short by 50.00 USDC: ask the customer for the rest, or accept it as settled." The line on the request page is marked as a status region, so screen readers can announce it when it changes (not yet tried with a screen reader). An empty filter now says what's empty and links to all requests; while requests load you see a "Loading requests" placeholder; and if requests can't be shown, the page says so in plain words and offers Reload, instead of a technical error. Nothing to do on your side.
- **One plain sentence per payment (part 1: the wording).** Each payment request now has one plain sentence saying what happened and what to do, e.g. "Short by 50.00 USDC: ask the customer for the rest, or accept it as settled." There is no countdown: Clearline only updates a real payment when you look it up again, so while one is confirming the sentence gives Ethereum's usual pace ("Ethereum usually takes about 2 minutes") and asks you to look it up again; it is not a promise of when yours will be done. The words say "confirmed", not "final", because Clearline doesn't yet use Ethereum's own finality signal. Also fixed: the request page said "about about 2 minutes" and called 12 confirmations "final for practical purposes". Nothing to do on your side.
- **The At a glance screen no longer says payments are paid automatically outside the demo.** Automatic marking is a demo-only rule, but the live screen showed a "Paid automatically" tile, a "time saved" estimate and the line "Every payment is matched, checked and explained". In live mode the tile now reads "Checked and ready to mark paid" (payments waiting for you), the time-saved estimate is gone, and the "I've asked for the balance" hint, next step and customer reply say a person marks the invoice paid: nothing is marked paid until you confirm. The demo keeps its wording, now labelled demo. Nothing to do on your side.
- **Demo payments and a first invoice-matching check (branch `data/demo-scenarios`).** The 20 demo
  payments now exist as a catalogue: all 20 are real public transfers (not Clearline customers),
  replayed offline from recordings, each paired with a clearly labelled sample invoice; a 21st "still
  confirming" slot is a gap (no recorded transfer). A test checks each expected outcome. On these 20
  the matcher auto-matched 6, none wrongly, but 6 is far too few to rule out a high error rate: up to
  about 4 in 10 auto-matches being wrong is not ruled out (95% Wilson 0%-39%; docs/MATCHING_EVALS.md §8).
  Six known differences are recorded as failing-on-purpose tests; no product behaviour changed.
