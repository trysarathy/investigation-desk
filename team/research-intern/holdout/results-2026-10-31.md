## Skill gaps (general; for the builder)
1. **Label hygiene for chained sources.** When a figure reaches it through a secondary summary, it
   tags the original source as verified, instead of "verified that the summary says X; original not
   opened".
2. **Staying inside the evidence given.** When a task defines the evidence base, it brings in figures
   and dates from its own stored knowledge. These are labelled, but they sometimes outweigh the evidence
   it was handed and are not reconciled with it.
3. **Working out "if true" consequences.** When a key date or outcome depends on an unverified claim,
   it dismisses the claim instead of stating what follows if the claim turns out true and how to check.
4. **Labels inside deliverable text.** Ready-to-use wording (slide text, spoken lines) can drop the
   labels and caveats from the analysis, and can pick one estimate where several conflict.
5. **Bottom line first under time pressure.** Time-boxed answers sometimes open with process notes
   instead of a two-line answer.
6. **Flagging self-asserted vendor claims.** Certifications and similar badges a company claims for
   itself are repeated without saying they are unchecked.
7. **Routing instead of drafting.** When it blocks proposed outreach copy, it tends to write
   replacement copy itself rather than sending wording decisions to the founder for approval.
