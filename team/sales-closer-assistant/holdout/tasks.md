# sales-closer-assistant: hidden holdout tasks (5)

Rules for every task:
- Every company, person, email and number in `fixtures/` is **fictional test data**. Do not search the web
  for them and do not treat any fixture as a real company. The fixture files are the only "primary sources"
  about these prospects; Clearline's own facts come from your normal sources (company rules, GTM rules, the
  enterprise pack, the claims audit).
- This is a test run: **nothing is sent, signed, booked or written to Company HQ.** Where you would normally
  queue something for approval or log it, show exactly what you would queue/log instead.
- Today is the date given in each fixture. Aashmiin is in Singapore (SGT) unless the fixture says otherwise.
- Write your answer to Aashmiin as you would in real work: what she should do, what you prepared, what is
  waiting for her decision.
