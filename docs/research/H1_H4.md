## What triggered this
The two advisors are Matt and Vishal of Protégé Ventures. They are **advisors, not customers**. They said they were "not too sure what you're building". Vishal asked: "Why fix this in the books at the end of the period instead of the moment the payment comes in?" His point is that ops and sales need to release goods once a payment lands [advisor, 2 Nov 2026, relayed by Aashmiin]. A separate treasury advisor gave this advice: stay quiet by default, integrate rather than add a dashboard, show status plus an ETA, prevent problems rather than explain them, and remember that users don't understand crypto [advisor, relayed by Aashmiin].

## H1 "Moment of payment"
**Who feels it.** The person who has to say "ship it". That means ops, sales or fulfilment staff, not finance [ASSUMPTION].
- Exporters and trading firms in SG, HK and UAE free zones, and Indian service exporters, paid in USDT by foreign buyers (segment S6 in `icp-and-segments.md`) [ASSUMPTION].
- OTC desks.
- Hosting/VPS providers and B2B SaaS that grant access on payment (segments S1 and S3) [ASSUMPTION].

The pain is sharpest where USDT lands **directly in the company's own wallet** with no gateway in between. A gateway already sends a status for every payment (see the table) [ASSUMPTION, based on the competitor table]. Tron carries about 30% of stablecoin supply [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-10-31, https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoincharts/Tron]. The packs believe much SG/HK B2B USDT moves on Tron [ASSUMPTION: unverified pack entry, market.md §4].

**How often.** It happens every time a payment arrives, so a business sees it as often as it gets paid. That is far more often than month-end [ASSUMPTION]. We have no measured frequency.

**What they do today** [ASSUMPTION unless labelled]:
- Look the hash up on an explorer by hand, or wait for a screenshot from the payer.
- Check the gateway dashboard or webhook status.
- Ask an OTC desk to confirm on chat.
- Wait for the bank credit after conversion.

The screenshot habit is risky. Exchange help pages warn about forged payment screenshots and fake "USDT" tokens that carry the same symbol [ASSUMPTION: search, Luno/Binance/OKX help pages, not opened]. Circle's own docs say a deposit sent on the wrong chain is "not credited and [is] permanently lost" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://developers.circle.com/cpn/stablecoin-payments/howtos/receive-stablecoin-payin.md].

**Public evidence that "tell me the moment it's paid" is a real need** (fiat, not crypto). Xero's product-ideas forum has an idea called "Notifications - Alert when an invoice has been paid". It has 205 votes and was opened on 4 Sep 2013. The stated purpose is "easier for them to order the product/stock … when they know that they have been paid". On 29 Sep 2026 Xero replied that a paid-invoice notification "isn't in our near term plans" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967115-invoices-quotes/suggestions/44960575-notifications-alert-when-an-invoice-has-been-pai]. Public comments on the same page:
- "doesn't have the surplus to order supplies until a client pays an invoice" (31 Oct 2026).
- One commenter built "Google Apps script … to send a google chats and email notification when an invoice is reconciled" (22 Sep 2026).
- "taking deposits for jobs that require materials to be purchased ONCE the deposit has been paid" (6 Aug 2026).

These are public posts and the posters are not Clearline customers. Note that "paid" in Xero only shows up after reconciliation, which is Vishal's point exactly [ASSUMPTION].

**What it costs.** No sourced numbers exist. [GUESS]:
- Each manual check takes 5–15 min (open the explorer, compare amount, contract and confirmations).
- The bigger cost is a day's delay to shipment, or releasing goods against a fake or short payment. One error can cost the whole order value. That is why this is a risk problem more than a time problem.

**Who pays.** The owner or COO for the ops benefit. The finance lead if the Xero entry is bundled in [ASSUMPTION].

**Why now.**
- B2B stablecoin payments were about US$226bn a year at end-2025 [ASSUMPTION: secondary, McKinsey/Artemis via Reap, market.md].
- India ranked first in Chainalysis's 2025 adoption index [ASSUMPTION: secondary, The Block via search].

**Counter-forces:**
- **UAE mainland:** under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation, onshore merchants may accept only licensed dirham tokens for goods and services. Foreign tokens such as USDT are allowed only for buying virtual assets [ASSUMPTION: secondary, CMS/Pinsent Masons via search]. That leaves UAE free-zone firms only, pending counsel.
- **India:** a payments-firm blog says USDT received into a wallet is not a valid export receipt under FEMA [ASSUMPTION: secondary, Xflow blog via search].

**What exists already** (table below):
- Every gateway (BVNK, NOWPayments, CoinGate, Circle, Stripe, Triple-A, Crypto.com Pay) already sends a paid/underpaid status for **its own** checkout. Most of them convert to fiat.
- CoinGate's "payment channels" give one permanent deposit address per client, plus "Webhook notifications … in real time" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://coingate.com/payment-channels]. That is H1 for anyone willing to receive through CoinGate.
- **The gap:** no tool we found watches a company's *own* wallet and sends "Paid: final, right token, right amount, safe to release" to Slack or email, then the Xero entry [ASSUMPTION: no such product found in this search; absence is not proof].

**Fit with what's built.** Strong engine, missing delivery.
- Built: the checks for token contract, amount, address and confirmations ("n of N … usually within {eta}") [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, docs/SPEC.md D02 on origin/main].
- Built: checking a payment by hash on Tron, Ethereum and BNB Chain [VERIFIED: build session 2026-10-31, docs/SPEC.md §3; src/lib/chain/tron.ts, evm.ts].
- Not built: address watching (a person pastes the hash) [ASSUMPTION: unverified pack entry, competitors.md].
- Not built: Slack and other outbound pushes; SPEC lists Slack under "Not in version 1" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, docs/SPEC.md].
- Payment requests work on Ethereum only [VERIFIED: build session 2026-10-31, src/lib/requests/check.ts:74].

**Proves H1 in 10 conversations** if all of these hold:
- ≥6 name a person who releases goods or access on payment.
- ≥5 receive into their own wallet (no gateway).
- ≥4 describe checking an explorer or screenshot in the last 30 days.
- ≥3 recall a delayed shipment or a short, fake or wrong-network payment in the last 90 days.
- ≥3 say where the alert should land (Slack, email or ERP).
- ≥2 accept the pilot price.

**Kills H1** if any of these hold:
- ≥6 already get the signal from a gateway or OTC desk.
- Release waits for the bank credit after conversion anyway.
- "We release before payment on credit terms."

## H4 "Current Clearline"
**Who feels it.** Finance leads, controllers or bookkeepers, and their outside accountants, at firms paid in USDC/USDT into their own wallet. Usually crypto/Web3 SaaS and agencies on Xero (S1, S2), plus accounting-firm partners (P1) [ASSUMPTION, icp-and-segments.md].

**How often.** Weekly to monthly, peaking at month-end close [ASSUMPTION].

**What they do today.** Export the wallet history to a spreadsheet, match payments to invoices by amount and date, chase the payer when a payment arrives short, then key a manual journal into Xero. Or use a sub-ledger. Cryptio, for example, exports "Manual Journals" and "Bank Transactions" to Xero [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://apps.xero.com/sg/app/cryptio].

**What it costs.** [GUESS] 15–30 min per exception plus close delay. The ROI default in the packs puts break-even at about 22 receipts a month [ASSUMPTION: unverified pack entry, icp-and-segments.md §3]. It is deferrable work, so the pain is real but not urgent [ASSUMPTION].

**Who pays.** The finance lead or founder [ASSUMPTION].

**Why now.** FASB proposed treating some stablecoins as cash equivalents; comments are due 19 Nov 2026 [ASSUMPTION: secondary, Crowe, opened by claims audit 2026-10-29]. GENIUS takes effect 18 Jan 2027 [ASSUMPTION: search, regulation.md].

**What exists already.** Sub-ledgers that push to Xero:
- Cryptio: "450+ enterprises", quote-only pricing [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://cryptio.co , https://cryptio.co/pricing].
- Bitwave: "pushes in deposits / withdrawals, bill / invoice payments" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://apps.xero.com/us/app/bitwave].
- TRES: uses Xero's "bank transaction, invoice, and bill APIs" [VERIFIED: research-intern 2026-11-02, https://apps.xero.com/za/app/tres-finance-web3-accounting].
- Request Finance: Xero/QBO/NetSuite sync for its own invoices [VERIFIED: claims audit 2026-10-29, help.request.finance accounting-integrations-overview].

**The gap:** plain-English explanations of wrong payments and SMB pricing. All four sell to crypto-native or enterprise finance teams [ASSUMPTION].

**Fit.** High. Matching, explanations and approve-then-post are built. Xero is in testing [VERIFIED: build session 2026-10-31, docs/ACCOUNTING_SYNC.md].

**Proves H4 in 10 conversations** if all of these hold:
- ≥6 receive ≥25 payments a month into their own wallet and use Xero or QBO.
- ≥4 can show a short, wrong-token or unreferenced payment from the last 90 days.
- ≥4 spend more than 2 hours at close on it.
- ≥2 accept US$300 for the pilot.

**Kills H4** if any of these hold:
- ≥5 already use a sub-ledger and are happy with it.
- Their volume is under 10 a month.
- "The accountant does it once a year."
