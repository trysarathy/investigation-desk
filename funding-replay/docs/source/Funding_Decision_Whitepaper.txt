FOUNDING WHITEPAPER           /   EXPERIMENT 01


When to fund a supplier payment
A read-only treasury experiment for Singapore importers paying US suppliers
Prepared for Aashmiin Kaur        •   3 November 2026

The decision at 3 pm
It is Friday in Singapore. A controller can fund a USD 100,000 supplier payment now, or keep the SGD in
the business until Monday. The supplier needs usable dollars on Wednesday. Before Friday’s cutoff, can
the controller show that Monday is actually an available option, that nobody else needs the same cash,
and that waiting is worth the risk?
Our proposed capability is a timestamped funding decision record. For one legal entity, obligation and
corridor, it shows the earliest binding constraint, the cash available after competing commitments, and
the cost of two admissible funding choices. It explains why to keep the existing instruction when
evidence is missing. A human uses the existing bank or provider to act.
Decision: test the information problem before building a platform. Financing an early supplier payment
is a different business. A faster rail is useful only if transit drives the funding deadline. The interviews
have not established which constraint dominates. This experiment is inexpensive enough to find out.

How to read the evidence
Verified means a supplied artefact, primary source or reproducible calculation supports the narrow
claim. Reported in interview identifies testimony. Assumption covers the proposed design, all synthetic
terms, prices and thresholds. Unknown marks an unresolved dependency. The payment narrative and
financial tables are constructed examples; none describes an actual customer transaction. External
reference links were checked on 3 November 2026.
For the founder: the experimental economics and stop conditions matter most. For treasury: inspect the
assumptions about available cash and cutoffs. For engineering: the decision record and failure tests
define the minimum product.

The control group
Before proposing a product, ask what the operator would have done with a shared calendar, AP export,
confirmed balances and an existing bank facility. Reproduce the rule exactly: applicable holidays,
maker/checker availability, funding lead, payout lead and an explicit safety buffer. Then test negotiating
a later contractual date. A negotiated term that removes the whole problem is a success for the
customer and a reason not to sell software.
Design decision. An early transfer is discretionary only when the buyer had an alternative under the
terms and evidence available at the earlier choice. A later favourable quote cannot justify a Friday
decision retrospectively. No deployment should advise waiting if the last safe baseline slot would expire
before its alternative becomes evidenced.

Where the modeled benefit disappears
Verified arithmetic on assumptions. The customer’s own money is tied up for 5 or 2 days. Simple carry =
135,000 × annual differential rate × days / 365. Three days at 8% produce SGD 88.77. After SGD 10 extra
processing, the conditional net advantage is SGD 78.77 before subscription. Restricted cash falls by
405,000 SGD-days; average restricted balance falls by SGD 13,500 across 30 days. Peak exposure on an
individual transfer does not fall.
Cost of each gap. Early and later use the importer’s own bank balance; the 8% is hypothetical avoided
borrowing or incremental return, not interest actually charged. The token route uses its own SGD for
one day; liquidity partners supply ramp inventory and price FX and conversion into separate charges. A
lender advances SGD 135,000 for five days with SGD 27,000 locked collateral. The borrower pays 12%
draw interest plus a fee; nominal unsecured exposure is SGD 108,000. The model does not assume a
lender will offer this facility.
Underwriting is real work. Illustratively, funding SGD 135,000 at 8% for five days costs a lender SGD
147.95. Under an assumed per-draw default probability of 0.1% and 60% loss severity on the unsecured
portion, its expected credit loss is SGD 64.80. The SGD 356.92 fee plus interest must cover both, capital,
operations and the adverse tail. Do not charge lender loss again to the borrower. The borrower’s
counterparty exposure line instead prices the risk to its posted cash.
Risk inputs, not measurements. Expected late cost: 0.2% × SGD 1,000; 0.5% on the token route.
Expected counterparty loss: 0.01% × 50% of exposed cash. Residual token slippage/depeg beyond
ordinary spreads: 0.25% × 1% of principal. Full quote incl. ordinary slippage, haircut, haircut changes and
the cost of a fall-back rail remain unknown. Reject any comparison that quietly reduces the level of
service.
The pivotal input is usable discretionary time at a real differential rate. Each avoidable day is worth
SGD 29.59 at 8%; break-even on the SGD 10 increment is 0.338 day, or 0.90% annual differential over
three days. Contractual buffer or idle cash at the same yield makes the incumbent SGD 10 cheaper. Just
5.84 bp worse FX, or 0.79 percentage points more late risk with a SGD 10,000 impact, erases the entire
advantage. Equal deadlines alone do not establish equal reliability.

The decision mechanism
Figure 2. Data and decisions only. This system reads a dated export, builds a view of the world as known
at a selected instant and produces a record. The user’s existing treasury controls remain responsible for
any instruction.




Inputs. For one legal entity and currency pair: invoice and net beneficiary deadline; source balance
snapshots; all other known reservations; forecast versus cleared receipts; FX rate, spread, fees and
expiry; operating calendars; contractual cutoffs and maximum last-mile time; authenticated capacity or a
documented fallback. Store event time and first-known time separately. A report generated on Monday
cannot become Friday evidence because it describes a Friday event.
Decision rule. Compare candidate slots only if in the allowed contract/calendar, source cash net of other
liabilities covers priced funding requirements, and destination capacity and remaining time cover the
same deadline. Missing, stale or contradictory mandatory evidence yields unknown; a known constraint
violation yields infeasible. The later option at Friday requires a credible forward commitment or a
protected alternative, not hindsight. Without it the safe answer is to keep the incumbent option or ask
for a binding offer.
Trust boundary. A bank snapshot proves a dated balance, not future cash; a provider acceptance proves
submission, not beneficiary credit. A merchant operator can document an override, but may not turn an
unsupported state into “verified”. In a pilot use read-only scoped exports, no account numbers in shared
demos, no signing credentials and separate consent for retention. Track “proposed → reviewed →
manually acted → externally confirmed”; last two require external evidence and are not implemented by
the replay.

Four incidents the happy path hides
Weekend or market closure: Friday cutoff passes; if Monday is closed the delayed slot is lost. Keep the
original funding instruction when delay cannot be protected. Receivable arrives late: classify the
forecast separately; reduce headroom only on committed outflows, increase usable funds only on settled
evidence. If cash is short, obtain an existing committed facility through the bank or report the shortfall.
Off-ramp failure: a landed token does not meet the USD obligation. Without confirmed alternate
capacity, mark deadline at risk; price emergency FX, network and late charges. Concurrent payouts:
subtract the second instruction and compare again. If there is a shared liquidity pool, reserve atomically
in any future live system; this single-obligation replay only tests supplied concurrent encumbrances.

obligation at two defined slots, emits JSON and renders a standalone HTML view. No bank connectivity,
credential, paid API or money movement. The 22 automated tests passed through the shipped entry
point and its underlying decision functions. This tests the mechanics, not predictiveness, demand, real
cutoffs or payout reliability.

Python 3.9+ and time-zone data. No third-party packages.
What is worth reusing. After independently framing the funding decision we inspected the existing

Who might buy and how the business could
grow
Assumption. Sell a four-week read-only replay to a controller responsible for repeat supplier payments
in one corridor. Offer SGD 500 flat, credited against an initial subscription if appropriate. Proposed
recurring price: SGD 270 per legal entity per corridor per month, with agreed volume and support limits.
Charge for a decision workflow, not a percentage of principal. The operator prepares the case; the
controller owns the budget.
At the modeled SGD 78.77 differential, four suitable payments/month yield SGD 315.08 before the fee
and only SGD 45.08 after it. Sixteen yield SGD 1,260.32 before the fee, but this is not a forecast:
overlapping commitments, an unused cash balance, hedging costs, lower marginal rates or a costly
reserved fallback can eliminate it. For a 2× buyer value/fee hurdle at SGD 270, require SGD 540 of
documented gross monthly value net of incremental handling and risk; roughly seven genuinely
comparable opportunities at the illustrative rate. Count separately evidenced labour savings once.
Bottom-up planning range [Assumption]. Suppose interviews ultimately identify 100–500 reachable
entities with this currency pair; 10–30% have repeated actionable timing choices; 10–30% of those buy.
At SGD 3,240 annual price, the arithmetic gives SGD 3,240–145,800 annual recurring revenue (1–45
buyers). The account counts and conversion rates are unmeasured scenario inputs, not a census or a
market valuation. Replace them with named eligible firms, dated outreach, observed workflows and
signed contracts. This corridor alone does not justify a venture-scale claim.
Unit economics. At SGD 270/month, an assumed SGD 15 data/hosting, 1.5 support hours at SGD 45 and
SGD 30 monthly amortised onboarding cost leave SGD 157.50 contribution, about 58%, before sales,
R&D, insurance and tax. At five support hours the same subscription contributes nothing. Price
contracted integrations or decline them rather than disguise services revenue as software margin.
Expansion test. Require renewals at measured positive contribution, verified partner lead-time data,
permitted reuse and independent evidence across three firms before a second corridor. A compounding
asset might be a library of contractual cutoff exceptions with permitted, de-identified realised-versus-
expected timings. Customers retain rights to their own records; providers must consent to reuse of their
terms. A bank or TMS can neutralise this advantage with native schedules, richer outcomes or bundled
funding. Pursue an API only when multiple partners request the same evidence contract.

Interview script and falsification criteria
Begin: “Walk me through the last payout for which you had to move cash earlier than you wanted.” If
there was none: “Walk me through your most recent overseas supplier payment, from approval until the
supplier could use the money.” Do not propose a tool until that reconstruction is complete.
Ask: What currency, amount or range, due date and time zone? Which term set the earliest and latest
funding dates? Who actually chose the date, and what did they know then? What restriction applied to
the balance? Was there a receivable or credit line, and was it usable? What did staff do when late? Who
paid interest, fees or penalties? Can we inspect a redacted instruction, dated term and evidence of
supplier availability? What could a spreadsheet or renegotiated term already solve? Only then show the
Friday screen and ask precisely which decision they would change.
Draft outreach for founder review. “I’m an SMU accountancy student studying how Singapore importers
decide when to fund overseas supplier payments. Would you or the person who prepares your payment
run walk me through one recent USD payout for 20 minutes? A case where your current process works
well is equally useful. No account numbers or client names are needed.” No messages have been sent.

 Proposed gate             Pass, pivot or stop

 Behavioural pass          Within 14 days two unrelated treasury owners share an authorised redacted week,
                           including original timestamps and cutoff evidence. Across the two, identify at least
                           three discretionary decisions.

 Commercial pass           One budget owner signs the SGD 500 four-week scope; agree a 2× monthly value/fee
                           target, ongoing staffing cost, and no known additional deadline misses before
                           expansion. Price, agreement and renewal are unproven.

 Safety stop               Any false reassurance, cash-floor breach or deadline miss attributed to waiting pauses
                           that rule. Track every abstention. Replay cannot prove noninferiority for rare failures;
                           continue in prospective shadow mode.

 Pivot                     If credit or contract fixes timing on most cases, stop selling timing software. Test
                           receipt release only with recent held orders; test FX records only with accountant-
                           supplied lots and a paid review use case.

 Evidence stop             If six qualified conversations yield no authority to change a date, or data access/paid
                           interest fails after a completed scoped offer, stop development. If six interviews were
                           not obtained, report recruitment failure rather than rejecting market demand.
