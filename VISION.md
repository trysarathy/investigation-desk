# Vision

Every stablecoin payment, anywhere, traceable and explained in seconds.

Start with payment operations teams answering “where's my money?” Join verifiable chain events to company records, preserve uncertainty, and give an analyst an evidence-backed next action.

## Defensibility

The durable asset is permissioned, reproducible outcomes: versioned evidence, analyst adjudication, regression cases and integration into operations. A UI, public-chain lookup or token is not a moat. Corrections are review labels, not automatically independent ground truth. Publish precision and recall only with denominators and label provenance; separate synthetic regression tests from real public-chain and private-ledger evaluations.

## Honesty constraints

Public transfers do not establish customer identity, intended network, minimum policy, ledger credit or bank delivery. Company Scan reports observed signals and unassessed checks, never calls all transfers uncredited. Face-value stablecoin amounts are not measured USD loss. Partial history is not a complete scan. Unknowns stay reviewable. No signing, custody, seed phrases or fund movement.

## Release gates

100+ independently labelled public transactions plus authorized ledger cases for company-dependent diagnoses; zero known wrong High results; measured cold and warm latency; tenant isolation; authenticated roles and audit; external security review and DPA before real customer data. Targets (<2s investigation, <60s scan) are targets until measured. Live integration installation and verified wallet ownership require partner participation.
